Privacy Policy

Effective Date: 31st July 2025
Last Updated: 31st July 2025

JK Maini Group and its affiliated companies ("JK Maini", "we", "our", "us") value your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide to us in accordance with applicable data protection and privacy laws.

This Privacy Policy (“Policy”) outlines how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your information through our website www.jkmaini.com ("Website") and through offline interactions related to our business operations in precision engineering and aerospace sectors.

IMPORTANT NOTE

  • You can browse our Website without providing any personal information.
  • We collect personal data only for specific business, technical, or legal purposes, and do not misuse it.
  • By using our Website or engaging with us through any medium, you agree to the terms of this Policy.
  • If you do not agree with this Policy, you should refrain from using the Website or sharing personal information with us.
  • We may update this Policy from time to time. It is your responsibility to check for changes.

Information We Collect

  • Personal Identification Details: Name, designation, contact number, email address, organization name, job title, location, and country of business.
  • Business Information: Company name, industry, job function, interests, and business needs when interacting with us.
  • Recruitment Information: Details submitted through the Careers page, including CV, education, employment history, skills, and certifications.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, OS, pages visited, time spent, referring URLs, and device identifiers.
  • Cookies and Tracking Tools: Used to understand user behavior and improve our Website. Cookie preferences can be customized in your browser.

How We Collect Information

  • Directly from You: Through forms, job applications, and communications.
  • Automatically: Using cookies, analytics, and server logs.
  • From Third Parties: Via service providers, analytics partners, or lawful industry sources.

Use of Your Information

  • To respond to inquiries, proposals, or project requests
  • To process job applications
  • To improve website performance and content
  • To manage vendor and partner relationships
  • To send updates and communications
  • To comply with laws and secure our systems

Data Retention

We retain data only as long as necessary, such as:

  • Until a business relationship exists
  • Until recruitment cycle ends
  • As needed for legal or regulatory compliance

Information Security

We use organizational and technical safeguards to protect your data. While no method is completely secure, we encourage users to protect their credentials proactively.

Sharing of Information

  • With trusted third-party service providers
  • Within JK Maini Group for business operations
  • With law enforcement or regulators if required
  • During mergers or restructuring with safeguards

We do not sell or trade personal information for marketing or commercial gain.

Your Rights

You may have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccuracies
  • Withdraw consent
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict processing
  • Complain to a data protection authority

To exercise these rights, email us at privacy@jkmaini.com. We may request identity verification before processing.

Third-Party Websites

Our Website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for their content or privacy practices. Please read their policies before sharing data.

Children’s Privacy

Our services are not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect data from minors.
