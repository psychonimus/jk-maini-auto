Key Account Manager - Marketing (Aero)

Bangalore, India Full Time 3–5 Years BE in Mechanical Engineering / Aerospace Engineering
Function : Sales and Marketing
Sub-Function : Marketing

About the Role

Business Development

The Key Account Manager – Aerospace is responsible for driving strategic business growth by securing new and existing customer orders, expanding the RFQ pipeline, onboarding new part numbers, and ensuring timely execution of RFQs, FAIs, and contractual obligations. The role demands strong tracking, data-driven decision-making, and seamless collaboration with internal teams to deliver on customer commitments.

Job Description

Key Responsibilities:
  • Win targeted annual business by acquiring new customers and growing existing accounts, with specific line item and revenue targets.
  • Build and manage a robust RFQ pipeline with real-time tracking and detailed loss analysis on Smartsheet (MIS software).
  • Selectively pursue RFQs that meet the minimum line item average while maintaining strong documentation and transparency.
  • Lead timely execution of FAI part numbers annually, ensuring contract reviews are initiated and live progress is closely monitored.
  • Adhere to contractual terms and timelines for pricing settlements related to raw materials, logistics, duties, and other commercial elements.
  • Maintain an organized, live repository of contracts and NDAs with complete traceability using Smartsheet.
  • Drive continuous learning, personal growth, and professional etiquette as part of performance improvement.

Financial Accountability:
  • No exclusive financial accountability.

Experience:
  • 3–5 years of experience in Business Development or Key Account Management, preferably in the Aerospace sector.

Technical & Professional Knowledge:
  • In-depth knowledge of Aerospace RFQ & quotation processes, including lifecycle management.
  • Understanding of FAI (First Article Inspection) processes and AS9100 Rev D documentation.
  • Expertise in costing, pricing, and settlement of raw materials like Titanium, Inconel, etc.
  • Proficiency in Smartsheet or other digital tracking tools for RFQ/FAI management and reporting.
  • Understanding of commercial contracts, NDA structures, and CRM tools like Salesforce, Zoho, or HubSpot.
  • Strong skills in root cause analysis, RFQ loss tracking, and data reporting.
  • Knowledge of international logistics, export documentation, ITAR/EAR compliance, and Incoterms.
  • Basic financial acumen including revenue targets, pricing models, and budgeting.
  • Understanding of manufacturing processes, CNC machining, and aerospace-specific requirements.

Personal Attributes:
  • Strategic thinking with the ability to pursue high-value business opportunities.
  • Strong analytical skills and attention to detail in documentation and pricing.
  • Excellent communicator with internal and external stakeholders.
  • High ownership and accountability for targets and deliverables.
  • Commitment to continuous learning and performance excellence.
